Style guide (Style A): cute chibi 3D toy-style render with a soft organic satin skin sheen: subtle soft highlights and visible fine scale or feather texture. Absolutely NO hard plastic or vinyl gloss, never glass-smooth; the skin looks naturally weathered and alive. Big round golden-amber eyes with white specular highlights, cheerful open smile showing tiny white fangs and a pink tongue, creamy white belly, bright yellow-orange claws (and small yellow-orange horn/nail accents where the species has any), pink inner ears, high-resolution digital art. Create a baby Tyrannosaurus rex in Style A. Anatomy (scientific reconstruction): a massive skull with strong jaws and heavy brows, tiny two-fingered arms, powerful long legs, thick heavy tail for balance. Body color: the entire body is fresh apple green with bone-armor etching lines. Expression: playful show-off roar. Pose/action: munching a snack with head bowed down, sitting flat on the ground in a wide squat, legs sprawled wide, tail lying limp and flat on the ground, cheeks stuffed full, body low and round. Tier 4 (gold-armored): skin pattern/tattoo: bone-armor etching lines. Gold-trimmed gear or weapon: a battle axe. Add subtle motion effect lines for energy. Weathering: a subtle dirt smudge on one cheek; a small mud clump on the tail tip. Composition rules: single creature only, whole body fully visible and uncut, centered horizontally, positioned so the lowest body part sits about 5% above the bottom edge of the frame, generous empty margins of at least 4% on every side, plain pure white studio background, no text, no logo, no watermark, no drop shadow. SILHOUETTE ENVELOPE (highest priority): fit the ENTIRE creature - head, neck, tail, limbs, props and any floating effects - inside an imaginary SQUARE box: overall width and overall height must be nearly equal, ratio strictly between 1:1.2 and 1.2:1. Prefer a squat wide stance over an upright narrow one; curl the tail and lower the head rather than letting anything stretch outside the square envelope. A narrow-tall silhouette (much taller than wide) is forbidden: if in doubt, make the creature squatter and wider. Follow the Style A description above exactly.
